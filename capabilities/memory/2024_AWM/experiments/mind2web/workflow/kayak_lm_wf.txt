## Summary Workflows:
## enter_search_location
Given that you are on the Kayak search page, this workflow enters a city, airport, or landmark into the location field and selects the autocomplete suggestion.
[textbox]  {location_field} -> TYPE: {location_name}
[span]  {location_suggestion} -> CLICK

## select_calendar_date
Given that you are on the Kayak search page, this workflow opens the date picker and selects a specific travel date.
[button]  {date_input} -> CLICK
[div]  {date_day} -> CLICK

## apply_amenity_filter
Given that you are on the Kayak search results or filter page, this workflow opens a filter menu and selects a specific amenity option.
[button]  {filter_menu} -> CLICK
[checkbox]  {filter_option} -> CLICK

## sort_search_results
Given that you are on the Kayak search results page, this workflow changes the sorting order of the results.
[div]  {current_sort} -> CLICK
[tab]  {desired_sort} -> CLICK

## set_flight_trip_type
Given that you are on the Kayak flight search page, this workflow changes the flight trip type (e.g. to One-way).
[svg]  {trip_type_menu} -> CLICK
[tab]  {trip_type} -> CLICK

## adjust_guests_rooms
Given that you are on the Kayak search page, this workflow increments the number of guests or rooms and confirms the selection.
[button]  Increment -> CLICK
[path]  {confirm_close} -> CLICK

## book_selected_option
Given that you are on the Kayak search results page, this workflow selects a specific price option and proceeds to view the deal.
[div]  {price_option} -> CLICK
[div]  View Deal -> CLICK
