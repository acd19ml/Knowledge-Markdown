## Summary Workflows:

## enter_flight_locations
Given that you are on the United Airlines booking or search page, this workflow enters the departure and destination city/airport for your flight.
[combobox]  Enter your departing city, airport name, or airpor... -> TYPE: {origin-city}
[button]  {origin-option} -> CLICK
[combobox]  Enter your destination city, airport name, or airp... -> TYPE: {destination-city}
[button]  {destination-option} -> CLICK

## select_travel_dates
Given that you are on the United Airlines booking or search page, this workflow selects the travel date(s) from the calendar.
[textbox]  {date-field} -> CLICK
[button]  {start-date} -> CLICK
[button]  {end-date} -> CLICK

## navigate_travel_info
Given that you are on the United Airlines homepage, this workflow navigates to a specific travel information page.
[tab]  TRAVEL INFO -> CLICK
[link]  {info-link} -> CLICK

## search_rental_car
Given that you are on the United Airlines homepage, this workflow initiates a car rental search with a specific pickup location.
[heading]  CAR -> CLICK
[combobox]  Enter pick up city, airport name, or airport code. -> TYPE: {city}
[div]  {city-option} -> CLICK
[button]  Find cars button. -> CLICK

## enter_trip_credentials
Given that you are on a trip retrieval or check-in page, this workflow enters the confirmation number and passenger last name to access trip details.
[textbox]  {confirmation-field} -> TYPE: {confirmation-number}
[textbox]  {last-name-field} -> TYPE: {last-name}
[button]  {submit-button} -> CLICK
