# Summary Workflows:

## search_and_select_location
Given that you are on the TripAdvisor search page, this workflow searches for a specific location and selects it from the suggestions.
[searchbox]  Where to? -> TYPE: {location}
[option]  {location_suggestion} -> CLICK

## select_date_range
Given that the calendar is open, this workflow selects the start and end dates for the travel or booking period.
[gridcell]  {start_date} -> CLICK
[gridcell]  {end_date} -> CLICK

## sort_by_price_low_to_high
Given that you are on the search results page, this workflow sorts the results by price from lowest to highest.
[div]  Sort by: -> CLICK
[div]  Price (low to high) -> CLICK

## select_filter_criteria
Given that you are on the search or results page, this workflow selects a specific filter option such as cuisine, amenity, or dietary requirement.
[checkbox/label]  {filter_name} -> CLICK

## submit_search_form
Given that you have filled in the search criteria, this workflow submits the form to retrieve results.
[button]  {submit_button_text} -> CLICK
